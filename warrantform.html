How To Apply For A Warrant

1. Gather Information

Ensure you have the correct date including day, month, and year. Gather all relevant information regarding the incident before beginning your warrant application.

2. Establish Probable Cause

You must have a valid reason for requesting the warrant and possess evidence supporting your request. Evidence will likely be one of the strongest components of your application.

3. Complete & Submit

Complete the appropriate warrant template and submit it to 901 B. Westbrook via Discord for review.

4. Await Review

If approved, wait for the completed warrant to be returned. Once returned, you are authorized to serve the warrant.
